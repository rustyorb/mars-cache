# Reference attribution and reuse

Third-party reference documents, illustrations, logos and data retain their original rights and terms. Publication in this collection does not relicense them.

Original documents retain their attribution and any source-specific notices. Publisher, edition, original URL and integrity information are recorded in `site/catalog.json` and `site/catalog.csv`.

- **CDC / HHS:** [Use of Agency Materials](https://www.cdc.gov/other/agencymaterials.html). Materials developed by CDC/HHS are credited per entry, are also available free from agency websites, and their use does not imply endorsement. Source documents are not substantively edited.
- **USGS:** [Copyrights and Credits](https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits). Credit: U.S. Geological Survey, U.S. Department of the Interior.
- **EPA:** [EPA Disclaimers](https://www.epa.gov/web-policies-and-procedures/epa-disclaimers). Source-specific notices remain applicable.
- **USDA / FEMA / National Weather Service:** original agency attribution remains with each reference; any third-party notices in individual works remain applicable.
- **Hesperian, regional university and state publications:** publisher links are provided; copies are not hosted in this public edition. Hesperian's [digital distribution policy](https://hesperian.org/open-copyright-policy/) requires permission for redistribution.

No agency or publisher endorses MARS CACHE. No source logos are used to brand this project.
