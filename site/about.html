Knowledge worth keeping.

MARS CACHE is the Mars brothers' collection of survival, repair and rebuilding references for Kentuckiana. MARS stands for Mobile Archive for Resilience & Survival.

Reading and downloading

The catalog contains 32 locally hosted references and 23 publisher resources. Hosted files can be read here or saved in the offline core download. Publisher resources open on their original websites and need internet access until you obtain your own copies. Search covers catalog descriptions, titles and publishers, not every word inside every PDF.

Unzip the offline download completely and open index.html. The bundled references work locally; publisher links still require the internet.

Sources and dates

Collection date: November 4, 2026. The regional topo selection covers parts of the Louisville area, not all of Kentuckiana. Publication dates vary and some references are historical. Static maps do not show current road, bridge, weather or fallout conditions. The collection has not undergone independent medical or engineering review.
