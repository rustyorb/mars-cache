MARS CACHE — Public Offline Core

Unzip the entire archive before opening index.html.
32 hosted references are included. The catalog also links to 23 publisher resources that require internet access until you download your own copies.

WHILE ONLINE: run Prepare-Personal-Cache.cmd on Windows, or python3 tools/prepare_personal.py on macOS/Linux. Python 3 is required. This downloads your own publisher files and health chapters directly into MARS-CACHE-Personal in your home folder. Check OFFLINE-READINESS.html there for missing files, then practice with Wi-Fi off.

Sources, audiences, dates and file hashes are recorded in catalog.json and catalog.csv. This archive contains no live weather, road or radiation data.
